Experience

Dec 2025 – Aug 2026

Visiting Student Researcher - Aerial Robotic Systems

NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory | Pasadena, CA

  • Developed a high-fidelity 6-DOF flight dynamics and thermodynamics simulator in MATLAB for a variable-altitude Venus Aerobot to predict trajectory and altitude control performance.
  • Integrated flight dynamics, envelope shape, a six-node thermal network, and gas transport into a single fifteen-state coupled system to model a Venus aerobot over full solar day missions.
  • Trained a neural-network surrogate of the envelope-shape boundary-value solver (1.1% worst channel error), achieving a 20,000× speedup and making multi-week 6-DOF flight simulations computationally feasible.
  • Implemented an Unscented Kalman Filter (UKF) leveraging existing avionics (barometer, IMU, thermistors) to perform state estimation and isolate specific-force deficits.
  • Designed a sequential change detection pipeline using a Cumulative Sum (CUSUM) test and constrained regression to detect leaks from 1 g/d to 100 g/d and separate them from sulphuric acid deposition.
